# Student Handbook

Short policy notes for the student support agent. The agent should retrieve one relevant section, not this whole file.

## Fee deadlines

Semester fees must be paid by the date shown on the student portal. A late fee applies after the due date.

## Payment portal failure

If the payment portal fails, the student may pay on the next working day without a late penalty. The student should raise a support request on the same day and quote the error.

## Scholarships

Merit scholarships are applied to the fee account only after approval. A scholarship belongs to one student.

## Course or programme change

A change of course or programme alters the official student record. It is allowed once per academic year and only before week 4 of the semester. The change is completed only after staff approval.

## Support requests

A support request is acknowledged within one working day. Use it for portal errors, document queries, and general help.
